# Mini-games

## Who's That Pokémon? — /guess

`/guess` starts a round **in a group**:

1. The bot posts a **black silhouette** of a random Pokémon with the
   question.
2. You have **20 seconds** total to type the correct name in that chat.
3. At **10 seconds** the bot drops a hint.
4. First correct answer wins **10 Forge Coins**; the bot reveals the
   artwork and closes the round.
5. One round at a time per group — starting a new one while a round is
   live is refused.

Only one game can run per group at a time, and answers are matched
against the Pokédex (close spellings still count).

## Other games

`/guess` is the only registered mini-game command. Older help text
mentions trivia, duels and slot machines, but those commands are **not
registered** in this build — if you were told about them, they are not
available.

The real "games" beyond `/guess` are the systems themselves: PvP battles
(`/battle`), gym leaders (`/gymbattle`), quests (`/quest`), achievements
(`/achievements`) and ranked seasons (`/ranked`).
