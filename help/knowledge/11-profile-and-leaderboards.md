# Profile, cards and leaderboards

## Your trainer

| Command | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| `/mystats` | Name, coins, catches and PvP at a glance |
| `/rename <name>` | Change your trainer name (2–20 characters) |
| `/botstats` | Bot uptime and aggregate activity stats |
| `/motivation` | A random motivational Pokémon quote |
| `/query <text>` | Report a bug or suggest a feature — sent to the owners |
| `/clear` | Unstick a frozen battle or trade |

Note that `/stats <name>` is a **Pokémon** stat card (e.g. `/stats
Charmeleon`), not your trainer stats — use `/mystats` for yourself.

## Trainer cards

- `/cards` — the card category menu. Pick a category, page through its
  templates, and equip one.
- `/editcard` — the contact sheet of every template plus every card
  colour, for quick comparison.

Equipped cards show up on your profile card image in battles, leaderboards
and wherever the bot renders your trainer card.

## Leaderboards — /leaderboard

`/leaderboard` (alias `/lb`) opens the board with **three tabs**:

| Tab | Ranked by |
| --- | --- |
| EXP | Trainer experience (default tab) |
| Pokémon | Number of Pokémon caught |
| Coins | Forge Coins held |

Each tab shows the **top 10** trainers. Tap a tab pill to switch in place.

## Season boards

- `/ranked` — your rank, RP and the season ladder.
- `/pvpleaderboard` — the current ranked-season standings.

## Troubleshooting your own profile

- Name not changing? It must be 2–20 characters.
- Command does nothing in a group? Try it in DM — several commands are
  DM-only.
- Stuck state? `/clear`, and `/cancel` to leave any wizard.
