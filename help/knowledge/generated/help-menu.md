# Forge — /help menu (as shown inside the bot)

The bot's built-in `/help` menu. Each module is a page of
commands with a one-line description and examples.

## Getting Started

### Basics

- `/start` — Begin or continue your journey
- `/help` — Open this menu
- `/mystats` — Name, coins, catches & PvP at a glance
- `/myinv · /myinventory` — Balls, stones, TMs & key items
- `/rename <name>` — Change your trainer name, e.g. /rename Ash
- `/query <text>` — Report a bug or suggest a feature, e.g. /query hunt lag
- `/clear` — Unstick a frozen battle or trade

### Regions

- `/travel` — Your regions & travel menu
- `/evtrain` — Pick a stat to EV train, then /hunt

Notes:

- regions unlock at trainer level = your gen number

## Catching & Battle

### Encounters

- `/hunt` — Wild encounter: fight it or throw a ball
- `/open · /close` — Hunt panel in your DM
- `/safari` — Safari Zone info
- `/enter <zone>` — Join a Safari zone, e.g. /enter Primal Ocean
- `/exit` — Leave the Safari Zone

### Battle

- `/battle` — Fight a trainer (reply to their message)
- `/challenge` — Challenge menu
- `/gym` — Gym league & leaders
- `/gymbattle <leader>` — Fight a gym leader, e.g. /gymbattle Brock
- `/analyze · /battleanalyze` — AI breakdown of your last battle
- `/zmine` — Equip a Z-Crystal (buy them in /shop → Z-Crystals)

Notes:

- catching keeps the Pokémon · winning battles pays 10–20 coins

## Collection & Data

### Browse

- `/mypokemons` — Paginated list of everything you caught
- `/mypokemon · /collection` — Quick list of your collection
- `/data <name>` — Species data & spawn info, e.g. /data gyarados
- `/details <name>` — Extended details, e.g. /details Charmeleon
- `/stats <name>` — Full stat card, e.g. /stats Charmeleon
- `/pc` — Storage box
- `/search` — Open collection search
- `/filter` — Interactive filter menu
- `/sort` — Sort options for your list
- `/myshinies` — Every shiny you own
- `/myfav` — Your favourites
- `/myforms` — Regional & alternate forms
- `/nicknames` — Pokémon you've nicknamed
- `/myanc` — Your Ancients
- `/lineage <name>` — Family tree of a bred Pokémon, e.g. /lineage eevee

### Manage

- `/nickname` — Nickname wizard (pick, then type)
- `/cancel` — Exit a wizard or dialog
- `/display` — Choose what list rows show
- `/setfav <name>` — Toggle favourite, e.g. /setfav pikachu
- `/release <name>` — Release a Pokémon, e.g. /release charmander
- `/forms <name>` — View & change forms, e.g. /forms pikachu

## Team Building

### Your Team

- `/myteam` — Team card image & roster
- `/teams` — List all your teams
- `/newteam <name>` — Create a team, e.g. /newteam rain
- `/delteam <name>` — Delete a team, e.g. /delteam rain
- `/team · /setteam · /remteam` — Legacy redirects to /myteam

### Train

- `/move <move>` — Change a Pokémon's moves, e.g. /move flamethrower
- `/bestnat <name>` — Best natures for a species, e.g. /bestnat garchomp
- `/chart` — IV/EV calculator
- `/evolve` — Confirm-screen evolution, e.g. /evolve bulbasaur
- `/checkevolve <name>` — Evolution paths, e.g. /checkevolve charmander

### Gear

- `/equip <item>` — Hold an item, e.g. /equip leftovers
- `/unequip <item>` — Remove a held item, e.g. /unequip leftovers

Notes:

- old /team · /setteam · /remteam redirect to the new system

## Shop & Items

### Rewards

- `/daily` — Daily reward (main group only)
- `/weekly` — Weekly reward (main group only)
- `/rewards` — Streaks & reward status
- `/redeem <code>` — Redeem a gift code, e.g. /redeem FORGE100

### Market

- `/shop` — Browse the market
- `/pballs` — List every Poké Ball
- `/buy <item> [qty]` — Buy items, e.g. /buy Ultra 2
- `/sell <item> [qty]` — Sell items, e.g. /sell candy 3
- `/use <item>` — Use an item, e.g. /use potion

### Training

- `/candy <name>` — Rare Candy +1 level, e.g. /candy pikachu
- `/vitamin <name>` — Boost EVs, e.g. /vitamin pikachu
- `/berry <name>` — Reduce EVs by 10, e.g. /berry pikachu
- `/boost <name>` — +1 IV once per stat, e.g. /boost pikachu
- `/nullcandy` — Reset a Pokémon to Lv.1

### Gifting

- `/give <coins>` — Give coins (reply to recipient), e.g. /give 100
- `/send_tm <TM>` — Send a TM (reply), e.g. /send_tm 05

Notes:

- /daily · /weekly are main group only · /candy is groups only

## Breeding

### Eggs

- `/daycare` — Drop off two Pokémon
- `/breed <p1> <p2>` — Breed a pair, e.g. /breed pikachu ditto
- `/eggs` — View your eggs
- `/hatch` — Hatch a ready egg

### Care

- `/walk` — Walk eggs to hatch faster
- `/warm` — Warm your eggs
- `/care` — Care for your eggs
- `/camp <name>` — Camp for 2 days to recover & bond, e.g. /camp eevee
- `/leavecamp <name>` — Recall it early, e.g. /leavecamp eevee

### Guide

- `/breedhelp` — Full breeding guide
